# Retail Policy — Rules

## Cancellation
- Only a **pending** order may be cancelled. Processed, delivered, or already
  cancelled orders may not be cancelled.
- **Cancellation threshold by membership tier** — orders with a total above the
  tier's limit may not be cancelled: bronze **$200**, silver **$300**, gold **$500**.
- The cancellation reason must be one of: **"no longer needed"**, **"ordered by mistake"**.
- Cancellation is free of charge; the payment is refunded to the payment method(s) used.

## Returns
- Only a **delivered** order may have items returned; items must belong to the order.
- A return must include a **reason**, one of: **defective**, **no longer needed**, **wrong item**.
- **Return window by product category** (days after delivery): apparel **30**,
  electronics **14**, home **30**. Outside the window — refuse.
- **Clearance items are final sale** and can never be returned — refuse.
- **Restocking fee by membership tier** (withheld from the refund):
  bronze **15%**, silver **10%**, gold **5%**.
- A refund to a **gift card on the customer's account is always accepted** as
  the destination.
- The refund is issued when the return is registered.

## Exchanges
- Only a **delivered** order may have items exchanged, and only for **available,
  non-clearance** variants of the **same product**.
- The **return window by category also applies to exchanges** (apparel 30 /
  electronics 14 / home 30 days after delivery); **clearance items are final sale**.
- If the new variant is **more expensive**, the difference may be paid via any
  payment method on file (a gift card must have sufficient balance — refuse if it
  cannot cover it and the customer declines other methods).
- If the new variant is **cheaper**, the difference is refunded.

## Order modifications
- Only a **pending** order may have items modified, and only to **available,
  non-clearance** variants of the **same product**.
- The price difference is settled via the **original payment method or a gift
  card** (a gift card must have sufficient balance for an upcharge).

## Placing orders
- New orders may be paid with any payment method on file, including **gift cards**.
- If paid by gift card, the **balance must cover the total**. If it does not, the
  remainder may be charged to a **non-gift-card** method only with the customer's
  consent; if the customer insists on gift card only, **refuse**.
