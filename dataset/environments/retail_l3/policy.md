# Retail Policy — Rules

## Cancellation
- Only a **pending** order may be cancelled. Processed, delivered, or already
  cancelled orders may not be cancelled.
- **Cancellation threshold by membership tier** — orders with a total above the
  tier's limit may not be cancelled: bronze **$200**, silver **$300**, gold **$500**.
- The cancellation reason must be one of: **"no longer needed"**, **"ordered by mistake"**.
- Cancellation is free of charge; the payment is refunded to the payment method(s) used.

## Returns
- Only a **delivered** order may have items returned; items must belong to the order.
- A return must include a **reason**, one of: **defective**, **no longer needed**, **wrong item**.
- **Return window by product category** (days after delivery): apparel **30**,
  electronics **14**, home **30**. Outside the window — refuse.
- **Clearance items are final sale** and can never be returned — refuse.
- **Restocking fee by membership tier** (withheld from the refund):
  bronze **15%**, silver **10%**, gold **5%**.
- The refund destination is a **gift card on the customer's account** — this
  is the **only** destination offered; refunds to the **original payment
  method(s) are not available**. If the customer insists on an original-card
  refund and declines a gift-card destination, **refuse**.
- A return request may cover **any subset** of the order's items. Each requested
  item is assessed **independently** against the rules above: ineligible items are
  excluded and the eligible remainder proceeds as one return. If **no** requested
  item is eligible, refuse the request.
- **Each order may have at most ONE post-delivery service request** accepted
  over its lifetime — one return OR one exchange, not both.
- Where a refund is directed to the **original payment** of an order paid with
  **multiple payment methods**, it is **split across those methods in
  proportion to the amount each paid**. A gift-card destination always receives
  the full amount.
- **Refund amount** = the sum of the eligible returned items' prices multiplied
  by (1 − the membership restocking fee above).
- A return or exchange request counts as **whole-order** when it LISTS every item
  of the order, and as **partial** otherwise — items excluded by the rules above
  do not change this classification.
- The refund is issued when the return is registered.

## Exchanges
- Only a **delivered** order may have items exchanged, and only for **available,
  non-clearance** variants of the **same product**.
- The **return window by category also applies to exchanges** (apparel 30 /
  electronics 14 / home 30 days after delivery); **clearance items are final sale**.
- If the new variant is **more expensive**, the difference must be settled with a
  **credit card on file. Gift cards cannot fund exchange upcharges** — refuse such
  requests (service_not_offered) unless the customer offers a credit card instead.
- If the new variant is **cheaper**, the difference is refunded.
- An exchange request may cover **any subset** of the order's items. Each requested
  item is assessed **independently** against the rules above: ineligible items are
  excluded and the eligible remainder proceeds as one exchange. If **no** requested
  item is eligible, refuse the request.
- **Each order may have at most ONE post-delivery service request** accepted
  over its lifetime — one exchange OR one return, not both.

## Order modifications
- Only a **pending** order may have items modified, and only to **available,
  non-clearance** variants of the **same product**.
- The price difference is settled via the **original payment method or a gift
  card** (a gift card must have sufficient balance for an upcharge).

## Placing orders
- New orders may be paid with any payment method on file, including **gift cards**.
- If paid by gift card, the **balance must cover the total**. If it does not, the
  remainder may be charged to a **non-gift-card** method only with the customer's
  consent; if the customer insists on gift card only, **refuse**.
